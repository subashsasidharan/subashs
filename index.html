About

I study how firms in emerging economies grow, go global, and innovate — and what holds them back.

I am Professor of Economics at IIT Madras. My research uses firm-level data, mostly from Indian manufacturing, to examine foreign direct investment and technology spillovers, the financing constraints of small enterprises, participation in global value chains, and the drivers of innovation.

Foreign direct investment

Spillovers from multinationals, outward FDI, and industrial agglomeration.

Global value chains & trade

Who joins GVCs, who survives in export markets, and why.

Small firms & finance

Credit access, gender and caste of ownership, informality, and MSME policy.

Innovation & technology

R&D, digitalisation, automation, and their effects on firms and workers.

Associate Editor, Asian Economic Journal (Wiley) and Indian Economic Journal (Sage).
